03Assessment

SL & HL Assessment Architecture

Written examination papers test Theme A and Theme B separately, complemented by the 30-mark individual computational solution.

Component Focus & Syllabus Scope SL Weight HL Weight
Paper 1 Concepts of Computer Science (Theme A)

Assesses A1 Computer Fundamentals, A2 Networks, A3 Databases, and A4 Machine Learning, plus the pre-released Case Study.

 35% 40%
Paper 2 Computational Thinking & Problem-Solving (Theme B)

Assesses B1 Computational Thinking, B2 Programming, B3 Object-Oriented Programming, and B4 Abstract Data Types (HL only) using Python.

 35% 40%
IA Internal Assessment: Computational Solution (30 Marks)

Individual software project assessed across Criterion A (Planning), B (Design), C (Development), D (Functionality Video), and E (Evaluation).

 30% 20%
