Two-Year Scope & Sequence: DP1 & DP2 Roadmap

Chronological sequencing across the two-year Diploma Programme at Emirates International School, synchronizing lesson plans, weekly pacing, and milestone deliverables.

DP1 • Class of 2027

Year 1: Foundations & Core Programming

Year 12
Term 1 September – October

B2: Programming Fundamentals & Python Constructs

B2.1 Fundamentals B2.3 Constructs Python

Environment setup (VS Code, GitHub), data types, string manipulation, conditionals (if/elif/else), repetition loops (for/while), functions, and tracing.

Diagnostic Coding Baseline • Week 4
Term 2 November – December

B1: Computational Thinking & B3: Single-Class OOP

B1.1 Thinking B3.1 OOP Basics Flowcharts

Problem decomposition, abstraction, flowchart logic, class structure, constructors, instance variables, encapsulation, and unit testing.

IA Problem Identification • Criterion A Launch
Term 3 January – March

B4: ADTs (HL Only) & A3: Relational Databases

B4.1 ADTs (HL) A3.1-A3.3 Databases SQL

Linked lists, binary search trees (BSTs), sets, relational database modeling, ERDs, 1NF–3NF normalization, and multi-table SQL queries.

Database Design Practical Challenge
Term 4 & Summer April – August

Year 12 Timed Assessments & IA Prototype Drafting

Summative Exams IA Criteria A–C

End-of-Year exams; client consultation, Criterion A planning, Criterion B system architecture & record of tasks, and first working prototype.

IA Criteria A–C First Draft Submission
DP2 • Class of 2026/2027

Year 2: Systems, AI, Networks & Finals

Year 13
Term 1 September – October

A1: Computer Fundamentals & A4: Machine Learning

A1 Systems A4 ML Core Logic Gates

Von Neumann CPU architecture, GPU functions, cache memory, logic gates, truth tables, OS scheduling, and introductory machine learning algorithms.

Paper 1 Diagnostic • Logic Circuits
Term 2 November – December

A4: ML Deep Dive, A2: Networks & Final IA Submission

A4.3 ANNs & CNNs (HL) A2 Networks IA Complete

Deep learning architectures, hyperparameter tuning; packet switching, TCP/IP, network security; final submission of IA Criterion D & E with video demonstration.

CRITICAL: Official IA Final Submission (Criterion A–E)
Term 3 January – March

Paper 3 Case Study Seminars & Full Mock Exams

Paper 3 Case Study Full Mocks Diagnostic Drill

Intensive analysis of pre-seen Case Study ("The Perfect Chatbot"), full mock examinations under real timed conditions, and past paper mastery drills.

Full IB Mock Examinations (P1, P2 & P3)
Term 4 April – May

Official IB External Diploma Examinations

IB External Papers Official Graduation

Final IB Diploma written examinations for Paper 1 (Concepts & Case Study), Paper 2 (Computational Thinking & Python), and Paper 3 (HL Only).

Official IB Examination Session (May)
