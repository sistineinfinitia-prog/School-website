IB DP Computer Science at EIS

We teach Standard Level (SL) and Higher Level (HL) Computer Science. The course covers computational thinking, system architecture, relational databases, networks, object-oriented programming in Python, machine learning, and the 30-mark individual programming project (Internal Assessment).
