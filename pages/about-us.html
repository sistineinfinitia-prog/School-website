Platform Mandate: Why We Built This System

IB Diploma Computer Science demands both theoretical command and practical algorithmic development. Fragmented materials degrade candidate performance. This portal unifies all coursework, revision, and planning into a synchronized academic engine.

Fragmented Traditional Study

Disconnected Materials & Static PDF Archives

  • Past exam questions detached from official mark schemes, leaving students uncertain about examiner mark allocation.
  • Coursework milestones and unit deadlines buried in email chains and disjointed drive folders.
  • Vague Internal Assessment expectations resulting in incomplete Criterion A success criteria and low algorithmic complexity in Criterion D.
The Unified EIS CS Portal

Synchronized Revision & Coursework Platform

  • Interactive topic-by-topic question drills with instant mark scheme criteria, command term tags, and trace-table solvers.
  • Real-time deadline tracking, assessment urgency badges, calendar synchronization, and teacher resource attachments.
  • Step-by-step IA engineering roadmaps for Criteria A through E with annotated code exemplars and verified rubric breakdowns.

Diploma Examination Mastery Roadmap

Structured milestones taking candidates to diploma examination mastery.

Year 1 • Term 1

Computer Architecture & Python Foundations

Mastering computer architecture (Theme A1), Boolean logic gates, CPU bus cycles, and foundational Python control flow with trace tables.

Theme A1 Theme B1 Diagnostic
Year 1 • Term 2

Systems Architecture & IA Inception

Network protocols (Theme A2), relational SQL databases (Theme A3), Criterion A problem definition, and Criterion B system decomposition.

Theme A2 & A3 Crit A & B UML Design
Year 2 • Term 1

Algorithmic Complexity & Prototyping

Object-Oriented Programming (Theme B2/B3), abstract data types (HL), Criterion C development, Criterion D testing logs, and Case Study seminars.

Theme B3 & B4 Crit C & D Case Study
Year 2 • Term 2

Evaluation & Examination Sprints

Criterion E software appraisal, full-length timed mock examinations across Paper 1 and Paper 2, and targeted mark scheme refinement.

Crit E Final Paper 1 & 2 Mocks IB Diploma
