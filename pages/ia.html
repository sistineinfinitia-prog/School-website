Criteria Checklist

Criteria A–E Assessment Rubric

Every Criterion A success criterion must be testable in Criterion B, implemented in Criterion C, demonstrated on video in Criterion D, and evaluated with client feedback in Criterion E.

Criterion Deliverables & Examiner Expectations Marks
Criterion A Planning & Problem Specification

Identify a real client or advisor, document evidence of consultation, justify why a computational solution is needed, and define measurable, testable success criteria.

 6 Marks
Criterion B Solution Overview & Record of Tasks

Maintain a chronological Record of Tasks and provide system architecture diagrams, data structure specifications, UI wireframes, flowcharts/pseudocode, and a comprehensive test plan table.

 6 Marks
Criterion C Development & Algorithmic Complexity

Present annotated screenshots of key algorithms, data structures, OOP/modular organization, input validation, and database/file persistence, explaining how each technique works and citing external libraries.

 12 Marks
Criterion D Functionality & Extensibility (Video)

Submit a 2–7 minute screen-cast video demonstrating every success criterion working live, including edge-case handling, data persistence, and code extensibility.

 4 Marks
Criterion E Evaluation & Future Recommendations

Evaluate each success criterion against the Criterion B test plan, include documented client feedback, and propose realistic technical improvements for future iterations.

 6 Marks
