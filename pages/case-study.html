RAKT Insurance & Conversational AI

Pipeline 1. NLP, Tokenization & Vector Embeddings
NLU / NLG

User prompts pass through lexical preprocessing, sub-word tokenization, and high-dimensional vector embedding before entering the transformer self-attention layers. Intent classification and named-entity recognition (NER) extract policy numbers, claim categories, and sentiment.

Training 2. Pre-training, Fine-Tuning, RLHF & RAG
Mitigating Hallucinations

Because LLMs predict next-token probabilities rather than verifying facts, they can hallucinate policy clauses. Developers mitigate this using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) against a vetted vector database and Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF).

Governance 3. Latency, Privacy, Bias & Human-in-the-Loop
Evaluation Questions

High-mark synthesis questions test trade-offs between cloud GPU inference vs. on-premise hosting, prompt-injection defense, GDPR data protection for insurance records, algorithmic bias, and escalation thresholds to human claims adjusters.
